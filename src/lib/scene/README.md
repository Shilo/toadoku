# Scene contract and review

The scene consumes the authoritative `Layout` cell rectangles and inward anchors. It never allocates cells or controls. `time` is active elapsed seconds from the shared lifecycle clock; the scene owns no timers, observers or listeners. Lazy theme definitions supply liquid, bubble and vessel renderers. The scene imports their types only. Fixed geometry and motion safety budgets remain theme independent.

The agreed approach is a small shared cubic network with equal physical junction trims. Three compound nonzero fills add joining pockets and subtract the displaced corners with reverse winding. No stroked seam patches, gradients, mask filters or raster artwork are shipped. A distance field renderer was rejected because extraction, topology and smoothing would add runtime work without solving the fixed allocation constraint. Nine rounded tiles were rejected because they create trays and disconnected channels.

The responsive partition protects the complete visible union `0.4487D + 1.43px`, including face, lip, selected lift, focus cues, digits, notes and independent floating. An additional 0.5px separates liquid boundaries from that union. Connecting edge controls stay within horizontal or vertical strips outside every protected face. At each four-group junction, a conservative empty disc is the minimum distance to any of the 81 face centers minus its protected radius. Every fillet control stays inside that disc, so the complete cubic hull is disjoint from all faces. Shared edge objects and reverse winding keep the three fills joined.

The old construction excluded full nominal anchors plus double motion reserves. On exact-square cells it limited the horizontal band to 1px and the fillet to 0.6px at every screen size. The current local empty-disc construction recovers diagonal water space without shrinking faces or moving labels, centers, targets or spacing. Unit checks require useful junction reach relative to cell diameter, rather than merely a positive radius.

Pond retains broad S flow on a 24-second period. Cauldron authors rounded two-cubic simmer lobes with a separate 12-second profile and asymmetric top-down menu pools. Its ring events use the same application active clock. At most four are visible; internal four-cell junction sites reserve the full bubble radius, independent drift and a further 1px away from every face. Ring radius stays at least 3px while thickness grows from and returns to zero. Cream annuli use a separate decoration token and a one-third dark center keyline, with maximum thickness 2.4px and a transparent center. There are still exactly three liquid fills, with no alpha tints, filters or extra timers. Menu sites reserve their complete maximum radius and drift outside the full title/art float envelopes and above every wet-vessel hull. Frozen WeakMap caches keep layout-only site proofs and sorting out of the frame loop. One loop per menu role avoids overlap cusps; landscape pools use the title-side corridor so the pot does not hide a liquid color. Every pale control hull stays beside or below the full title. Ground paints after liquid/bubbles. Reduced motion holds the deterministic time-zero pose; existing hidden-page clock handling pauses all scene work.

The third ground color is a narrow closed band interpolated between corresponding wet/dry cubic controls. It stays inside the existing bank, at most 2px wide for Cauldron and 1.5px for Pond, with no extra wave or allocation change. Degenerate banks omit the band safely. The optional rim path and required liquid/vessel hooks belong to lazy theme definitions; shared Scene has no runtime Pond import or theme-ID branch.

The lead challenged the initial fixed shore dimensions: nominal 14px wet bank and 18px gameplay crest do not fit an 8px gap by themselves. The implementation derives the last full nominal leaf envelope plus 1.5px from the supplied anchors, then clamps all wet/dry hull controls between that envelope and the first dock control. This protects the combined independent extrema, even at a 28px leaf anchor. The Pond shore stays fixed. Its dry hull is wholly before the control edge, so all controls lie on dry ground. Cauldron uses one exact quadratic pot arc, represented as a single cubic with constant concavity, a lower center and raised ends. Its visible control hull is bounded by the actual face union and unchanged control allocation. Straight offscreen continuations follow the endpoint tangents. In short landscape menus only, the unchanged provisional artwork may straddle the rim above the SVG; the full title, controls and all bubble/art separation remain protected. Other menu allocations retain full art/ground separation. Landscape menu cove geometry is separate from gameplay and reserves the maximum panel from the first layout.

The lead's first integrated browser review found axis-aligned menu ledges at the content boundary. The menu paths now continue their interior endpoints using tangent-matched exterior segments. A later API revision exposes every actual wet/dry cubic control, including the reflected control previously implicit in an SVG `S` command. This permits complete hull checks rather than trusting a string-coordinate subset. A narrow landscape cove handle is clamped left of More's allocation.

A final contrast review found the cream title crossing shallow water. The lead moved title/art allocation into the authoritative layout contract. Pale menu lobes now use monotone-y cubic chains around the title rectangle expanded for the independent 1.8px float. Their title-band control hulls remain outside the title's horizontal range for every real-valued water phase; pre/post hulls remain above/below it. Pre/band/post joins have matching vertical derivatives. Below the title, the pale edges broaden into the pond. Deep/middle water remains beneath the title, preserving its cream Chewy treatment and the three-color palette without outlines, masks or rectangular panels. The selected curves are an accessibility correction to the established scene, not a new visual experiment. [WCAG contrast guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) requires 3:1 for large text; the theme's existing deep/middle contrast values meet that threshold.

The same review exposed title/shore clearance at short landscape heights. The left cove bank and crest now shrink to the actual gap between the full title float envelope and maximum menu panel. Every relevant wet/dry cubic hull stays in that gap, while the panel remains on dry ground. This is an all-phase construction bound as well as a fresh regression test.

The lead rejected the first contrast-correct desktop raster: a single tiny pre-title handle had also constrained the lower joins, yielding straight diagonals and sharp-looking turns despite exact C1 continuity. Revised curves use separate physical handle lengths for the before-title, after-title and post-title joins. The lower turns retain multi-pixel rounding independently of the near-top title clearance. Visible exit bends end just beyond the safe-area bleed, then continue horizontally outward; enlarging exterior bleed no longer distorts the visible bend. Focused tests enforce meaningful lower handle lengths, but final curve quality remains a separate raster acceptance decision.

The lead accepted the lower turns but rejected a residual top-right beak. The original right pale-lobe start was still much farther right than its title-band endpoint despite only a few pixels of available height. Both starts now clamp their horizontal travel to half the actual pre-title vertical span. A quantitative test enforces this bound; the title corridor and matching join derivatives are unchanged.

Fresh validation uses independent winding probes, complete swept visible rims, unchanged nominal Pond shore reserves, every wet/dry control-hull bound, full dock/maximum-menu/More ground coverage, repeatability and isolated geometry typing. Raster seams and perceived curve quality additionally require the lead's integrated browser review. These checks are unrelated to the prototype's historical sample counts.
