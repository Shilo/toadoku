# Progression terminology

Naming proposals only. The label Cycle was rejected; no replacement is selected. Rankings are design judgments based on clarity, playfulness, frog-world fit and localization, not measured user preferences.

## Incremental-game references

| Game | Terms in its official materials |
| --- | --- |
| Clicker Heroes | Ascension, Transcendence, Hero Souls, Ancient Souls |
| Cookie Clicker | Ascension, prestige levels, heavenly chips |
| Realm Grinder | Reincarnation, Ascension |
| AdVenture Capitalist | Angel Investors, Claim Angels |

Sources: [Clicker Heroes](https://blog.clickerheroes.com/understanding-clicker-heroes-mechanics-heroes-and-strategies/), [Cookie Clicker game source](https://orteil.dashnet.org/cookieclicker/main.js), [Realm Grinder developer announcements](https://steamcommunity.com/app/610080/allnews/), [Hyper Hippo support](https://screenzilla.helpshift.com/hc/en/5-adventure-capitalist/faq/33-why-would-i-use-gold-to-claim-angels/).

These are reference terminology, not mechanics to copy. Ascension and prestige often accompany lasting benefits in these examples; Toadodu's current scope adds earned currency, without upgrades or power changes.

## Ranked names for the repeat counter

| Rank | Name | Main tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Rebirth | Clearly communicates starting again and feels playful; may suggest transformation |
| 2 | Ascension | Recognizable incremental-game term; can imply power growth |
| 3 | Adventure | Warm and inviting; can imply different scenery or a story |
| 4 | Reincarnation | Explicit restart metaphor; long and more spiritual |
| 5 | Prestige | Familiar to genre players; less obvious outside the genre |
| 6 | Renewal | Gentle and clear; less energetic |

Journey and Lap are additional alternatives: Journey is calm but less direct about restarting, while Lap is short but sporty. Avoid Season because seasonal themes already use that meaning.

## Ranked level names

1. **Level:** most immediately understood and compatible with traditional Sudoku.
2. **Stage:** familiar game terminology, but can sound like a whole difficulty bracket.
3. **Leap:** strongest frog character, with a small discoverability cost.

Avoid Lily Pad for levels because it could also refer to board cells or decorative objects.

## Ranked currency names

1. **Dewdrops:** cute wetland imagery and a simple droplet icon.
2. **Lily Coins:** clearly spendable, with a recognizable frog-world association.
3. **Pearls:** short and valuable-sounding, but less specifically frog-themed.
4. **Pond Coins:** understandable but less distinctive.
5. **Fireflies:** colorful, but treating living creatures as spendable currency can feel awkward.

## Recommendation

**Rebirth · Level · Dewdrops.** Example: Rebirth 2, Level 7, 240 Dewdrops. The exact display and counter starting convention await the label choice; do not silently change saved numbering when a label changes.

Independent art-oriented alternative: Adventure · Leap · Dewdrops. Prefer Rebirth and Level for clearer reset meaning and familiar navigation; no story or changing scenery has been approved.

Proposed translations: Rebirth / Wiedergeburt / Renacimiento; Level / Level / Nivel; Dewdrops / Tautropfen / Gotas de rocío. These need final wording review. Compact displays can use a currency icon and amount with a fully localized accessible label.
